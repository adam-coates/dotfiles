# Sleep

About 90 minutes per cycle, four to six cycles a night.

![[Attachments/sleep-stages.svg]]

## Stages

1. **N1**: drifting off
2. **N2**: spindles and K-complexes
3. **N3**: slow waves, early in the night
4. **REM**: late in the night

## What it does

- [[Memory#Consolidation]]
- Clearing waste
- Regulating mood

half-life of caffeine:: 5 hours
