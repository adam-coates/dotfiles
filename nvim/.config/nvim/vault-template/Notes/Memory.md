# Memory

How experiences become something we can use later. See [[Sleep]] for the night shift and [[Spaced repetition]] for what to do about it.

## Encoding

Attention decides what gets in. The [[Hippocampus]] binds what, where and when. ^encoding

## Consolidation

New memories are fragile. During [[Sleep]], the [[Hippocampus]] replays the day, and the cortex slowly takes over. *Slow oscillations*, *spindles* and *ripples* line up for this.[^1]

## Retrieval

Remembering changes the memory: each retrieval is another round of consolidation. That is why testing beats rereading, and why [[Spaced repetition]] works.

[^1]: Diekelmann & Born (2010) is the classic review.
